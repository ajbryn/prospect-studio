# Example segments: aerial work platforms (scissor & boom lifts)

Use these as a starting point. Adapt them to the user's product and territory, and confirm with the user.

| Segment | Why they need lifts | Search keywords | Size hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warehousing, 3PL, distribution | Racking install and maintenance, high-bay lighting, inventory at height | distribution center, fulfillment center, 3PL, warehouse, cold storage*, cross-dock | 20+ employees |
| High-bay manufacturing & fabrication | Plant maintenance, cranes, lighting, overhead utilities | fabrication, manufacturing plant, machine shop (large), steel, assembly | 20+ |
| Electrical contractors | Commercial installs at height every day | commercial electrical contractor, electrical services | 20+ |
| HVAC / mechanical contractors | Rooftop and ceiling-mounted units in commercial buildings | commercial HVAC, mechanical contractor | 20+ |
| Sign, lighting & glazing contractors | Sign installation, parking-lot lighting, curtain wall | sign company, sign installation, glass & glazing, curtain wall | 10+ |
| Rack installers & material-handling integrators | Their job is installing racking at height | rack installation, pallet racking, material handling | any |
| Facilities & property management | Atriums, lobbies, campus maintenance | facility services, property management (commercial), building engineering | 50+ |
| General contractors (commercial/industrial) | Tilt-wall and warehouse construction | general contractor tilt-wall, industrial construction | 20+ |

\*Cold storage needs cold-rated equipment: note it as a caution.

**Facility cues** (for facility fit): clear height, high-bay, racking, dock doors, mezzanine, crane, fabrication bay, tilt-wall, square feet, rooftop units, installation, "work at height".

**Default exclusions:** equipment rental companies (competitors or channel), our own dealers, existing customers (suppression list), residential-only contractors, retail storefronts, offices without facilities.
