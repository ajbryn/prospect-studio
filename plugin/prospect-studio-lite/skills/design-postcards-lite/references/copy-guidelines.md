# Copy guidelines

- **Public, business-level facts only:** expansion, permit, new site, hiring, location, industry. Never personal details about employees.
- **Never negative:** no safety incidents, OSHA citations, lawsuits, layoffs or financial trouble, even if the research found them.
- **No invented offers.** Discounts, prices, financing and incentives come only from the user or `brand.json`.
- **No false familiarity:** don't write "As your trusted partner…" to a company that isn't a customer.
- **Dealer = local help:** the national brand leads on the front; the back names the local dealer as the contact.
- **Specific beats clever:** "Congrats on the 180,000 sq ft addition in Katy" beats "Big things are happening!"
- **One idea per card:** headline, one supporting line, one clear action (scan the QR code or call the dealer).
- **Plain words, short sentences.** Headlines in sentence case, ≤ 8 words where possible.
- **Tone options:** bold (confident, short), friendly (congratulatory, warm), technical (specs, capability), premium (calm, service-focused).
